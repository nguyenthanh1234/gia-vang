Privacy Policy

Updated 24 November 2026

This policy applies to the app Ô Ăn Quan - Cờ Dân Gian (package vn.codangian.o_an_quan) on Google Play, published by the developer Quê của tôi.

The app needs no account, shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking and requests no sensitive permissions. Playing against the computer and two players on one phone work offline.

Online play

Only when you choose online play does the app connect to the game server and send: the game type, the room rules, the nickname and quick phrases picked from a fixed list, and the moves of that match. There is no free text. A match is stored temporarily so two people can play and is deleted automatically after the room becomes inactive. The game server does not store IP addresses; the hosting provider may keep technical connection logs for operation and security. All connections use HTTPS.

Data stored on the device

Settings, the chosen nickname and win/loss counts are kept only in the app's storage on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app. If Android backup is on, the system may back them up to your own Google account.

Sharing

The invite button only opens the phone's share sheet with the room number; you choose where to send it. The app does not sell data or transfer it to third parties for advertising.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Online chat is limited to preset phrases.
